HPPD (aka. Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder)
-------------------------------------------------------

Hallucinogen persisting perception disorder (HPPD) is a
rare medical condition where a person experiences
ongoing or repeating visual disturbances and perceptual
distortions long after the effects of a psychedelic drug
have worn off.


Common Symptoms


Visual snow: A grainy, static-like layer over vision,
similar to a fuzzy television screen.

Trails and afterimages: Moving objects leave a blur or
repeating trail behind them (palinopsia).

Halos or auras: Glowing rings or bright borders around
objects and lights.

Altered colors: Colors appear intensely bright,
enhanced, or shifted.

Geometric patterns: Seeing faint fractals or shapes
on flat surfaces.
